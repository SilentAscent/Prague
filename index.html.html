How to play.

Keep it simple. The kids do not need to understand every section. Just follow these three steps.

1

Tap what you find

As you walk around Prague, tap the simple things the kids spot: clocks, statues, trams, swans, towers, desserts, funny photos, and hidden details on houses.

2

Give awards

At the end of the day, give each child awards like Dessert Champion, Best Map Helper, Funniest Photo, or Prague Quest Champion.

Adult shortcut

  • Use Find while walking around Prague.
  • Use Stamps and Awards at the end of the day.
  • Everything saves only on the phone being used.

Look up at the houses

Prague streets are full of little clues on buildings: carved animals, old house signs, painted symbols, tiny statues, door knockers, plaques, crests, and faces above windows. Many old houses used pictures instead of numbers, so kids can hunt for these like secret badges.
